## Themes

**Calibration fixture stability.** The block contains a deliberately unsolvable test case: a fixture that self-declares as permanently incomplete and is explicitly designed by the rubric to yield the same finding (evergreen-gap) every round. Both reviews correctly identify and report this permanent finding. No oscillation, no new issues, no regressions—this is steady-state behavior for a calibration exercise.
